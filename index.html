rclone Google Drive access

This is a personal app that lets the owner use rclone, an open-source command-line tool, to copy, sync and back up files in their own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Connects to the owner's Google Drive after they sign in and approve access.
  • Lists, uploads and downloads files only when the owner runs an rclone command.

Who uses it

Only the owner. The app is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.

Your data

Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive. Nothing is collected, stored or shared with anyone else. Read the privacy policy for details.
